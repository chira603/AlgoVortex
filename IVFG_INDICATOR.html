USAGE NOTES

Recommended usage

The AlgoVortex XAUUSD bot is designed to be self-sufficient on a single timeframe — attach it to a 45m, 1H, or 4H chart and it doesn't wait on any other chart to confirm. Shorts carried a lower win rate but a higher average payoff in this dataset, so size accordingly and keep the auto-plotted SL/TP intact rather than adjusting manually.

How signals are plotted

  • Long/short markers plot automatically the moment that chart's own signal fires — no second timeframe has to agree.
  • Every entry is bracketed with a stop-loss and take-profit at open, no manual placement required.
  • Exits fire either at TP/SL or when that same timeframe's signal flips against the open position.
