SESSIONKING NOTES

Session-based logic

The SessionKing engine uses session liquidity and POC (Point of Control) as its primary triggers. Each signal is plotted when price interacts with the session's fair value area — no confluences required. Shorts have a lower win rate but larger average wins in this dataset, so sizing matters.

How signals are plotted

  • Long/short markers are generated at POC extremes once liquidity is swept.
  • Every entry is bracketed with a fixed stop-loss and take-profit based on session volatility.
  • Exits are triggered either at SL/TP or when the session's liquidity profile shifts.
