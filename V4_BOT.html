How It Trades

A 100-leg grid, scaled by trend and news filters

V4 BOT runs on XAUUSD around the clock, adding legs at widening distances with rising lot sizes as price moves against the basket, and closing the basket on a shared take-profit or an equity stop.

24 / 7 Market Coverage

The engine watches XAUUSD continuously across all sessions, layering entries (Entry, Scalp, Hedge, Pivot, Swing, Momentum legs) as price develops.

100-Leg Grid With an Equity Stop

Up to 100 legs can stack at increasing lot sizes (0.01 up to several lots) and widening distances (1,000–5,000 points) as price moves against the basket. There is no per-trade stop-loss — the basket is protected only by a shared take-profit and an account-level equity stop that closes everything if floating loss reaches 50% of equity.

Escalating, Not Fixed, Sizing

Unlike a fixed-lot system, each new leg is larger than the last. This is what produces the smooth balance curve on the Charts page — but it also means a single adverse trend can require far more margin and floating drawdown than the leg count alone suggests.

EMA Trend & News Filters

A 200-period EMA filter and a news-event filter (pausing 30 minutes before and 10 minutes after high-impact USD news) gate when new legs are allowed to open.

Risk disclosure. This report summarizes an MT5 strategy-tester simulation of V4 BOT on XAUUSD (H1, 1 Jan – 24 Aug 2026, 100% real-tick history). The backtest was run with $600,000 of test capital and fixed lot sizes (0.01 up to several lots per leg) sized for that capital — it is not scaled to the requested live fund of $5,000. A 100-leg grid strategy like this has no per-trade stop-loss; drawdown is bounded only by a basket take-profit and a 50%-of-equity account stop. On a materially smaller account, the same lot schedule would consume available margin and reach that equity stop far sooner, so the balance curve, drawdown percentages, and win rate on this page should not be read as representative of how the strategy would behave on the $5,000 live account without re-sizing the lot schedule to that capital. Simulated and backtested results do not account for slippage, liquidity, or broker-execution differences that can occur on a live account, and past performance — simulated or live — is not a guarantee of future results. Grid/martingale-style strategies carry a risk of large, rapid drawdowns and total loss of the account in strongly trending markets; only trade with capital you can afford to lose, and treat every figure in this report as historical, not predictive.
